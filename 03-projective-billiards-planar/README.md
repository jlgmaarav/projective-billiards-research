# Reflective projective billiards in the plane

**Status:** Preprint available at [arXiv:2609.28817](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28817).

This folder contains a self-contained manuscript snapshot compiled on 3 October 2026, plus the verifier scripts associated with the paper. The snapshot is not necessarily text-identical to the arXiv version.

- [Manuscript PDF](main.pdf)
- [LaTeX source](main.tex)
- [Computational verifiers](verificadores/)

The manuscript studies polygonal projective billiards with a constant reflection law on each side. It gives a family with open sets of periodic trajectories for every period (n\geq 3), including odd periods, and develops classification and metric-rigidity results.

The verifier scripts check specific calculations and constructions; consult their headers for assumptions and invocation details. They supplement, and do not replace, the proofs in the manuscript.
